About Sudha

I'm a passionate painter creating handmade, framed artworks. I explore themes ranging from divine figures and spiritual icons, to the beauty of nature and the human form. Each piece is carefully crafted with love and attention, bringing color and life into every stroke.

My work is rooted in traditional techniques, using oil and acrylic paints to capture emotion, light, and texture. Every painting is unique — a reflection of my imagination and dedication. I also take custom commissions, creating personalized artworks to bring your vision to life.
